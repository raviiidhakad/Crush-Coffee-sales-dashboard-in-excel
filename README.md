# ☕ Crush Coffee Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Summary
Analyzed **149K+ transactions** across 3 NYC locations to identify key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, and operational inefficiencies.

Built an interactive Excel dashboard to support **data-driven decisions in staffing, pricing, and promotions**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Crush Coffee needed visibility into:
- What drives revenue growth
- When peak demand occurs
- Which products and locations perform best
- Where revenue opportunities are being missed

---

## 📌 Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue:** $698.8K  
- **Total Transactions:** 149,116  
- **Average Order Value:** $4.69  
- **Avg Items per Order:** 1.44  

---

## 🔍 Approach
Performed exploratory data analysis across 5 dimensions:
1. Monthly revenue trends (growth analysis)
2. Product/category performance (portfolio insights)
3. Hourly & daily sales patterns (operations planning)
4. Store-level comparison (location performance)
5. Order size distribution (upselling opportunities)

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 🚀 Revenue Growth
- Revenue increased **104% in 6 months**
- Significant growth observed from March onward
- February dip flagged as a potential seasonal or operational risk

### ☕ Product Performance
- Coffee contributes **38.6%**, Tea **28.1%** of total revenue
- Top products:
  - Barista Espresso ($91.4K)
  - Brewed Chai Tea ($77K)
- No single product dominates → **healthy diversification**

### ⏰ Peak Hours
- **8 AM – 10 AM generates 36% of daily revenue**
- Revenue drops significantly post 11 AM
- Morning = highest business impact window

### 📅 Customer Behavior
- **Weekdays contribute 72% of revenue**
- Saturday has lowest footfall → underutilized opportunity

### 📍 Store Performance
- All 3 stores perform nearly equally (~$230K each)
- Indicates **consistent operations but no standout location**

### ⚠️ Data Quality Issue
- **30.7% revenue tagged as "Not Defined" size**
- Limits pricing and upselling strategy

### 💰 Revenue Opportunity
- Low average order value ($4.69)
- Customers typically purchase **only 1 item**
- Indicates weak bundling/upselling

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Optimize Morning Peak (High Impact)
- Increase staffing during 8–10 AM
- Introduce **combo offers (coffee + bakery)**
- Reduce service delays during peak hours

### 2. Fix Data Gaps
- Resolve "Not Defined" size issue ($214K revenue impact)
- Improve POS data tracking for better analysis

### 3. Boost Weekend Sales
- Launch targeted weekend promotions
- Increase Saturday footfall through campaigns/events

### 4. Investigate February Dip
- Analyze seasonal or operational causes
- Prevent recurrence with proactive planning

### 5. Increase Average Order Value
- Introduce bundle pricing strategies
- Train staff for upselling (add-on products)
